Choose a platform by how well it supports the community's operating model, not by feature count.

## Prepare before the market scan

Define purpose, members, tasks, current channels, obligations and accountable owners. Rank requirements. Identify unacceptable business models or data practices.

## Compare with scenarios

Ask suppliers to demonstrate complete journeys for a new member, publisher, group coordinator, moderator and departing administrator. Include mobile, accessibility, errors and support.

## Score the whole service

Assess:

- information architecture and search;
- membership, roles and permissions;
- communication and knowledge;
- participation and moderation;
- privacy and data minimisation;
- security evidence;
- accessibility evidence;
- integrations and supplier dependencies;
- export, portability and exit;
- implementation, support and total lifecycle cost.

## Verify claims

Request documentation and current evidence. Check certification scope and dates. Put material promises into the agreement. Record gaps and roadmap dependencies separately from existing capability.

## Include the people affected

Test a realistic configuration with representative members, disabled people, administrators, moderators and support teams. Assess task success and understanding, not only visual preference.

## Decide with exit in view

Review export formats, migration support, deletion and contract termination before purchase. A platform should earn renewal through useful service rather than artificial lock-in.
