Government communities can support advisory groups, programs, grants, volunteers, professional networks and place-based participation. This guide is general and does not replace agency policy, legal advice or a formal assurance process.

## Define the official relationship

State the public purpose, accountable agency area, members, duration and decisions supported. Explain whether participation is voluntary and whether portal activity forms part of consultation, a formal application, a public record or none of those.

List channels the portal does not replace: agency website, emergency warnings, statutory notices, complaints, service transactions or records systems.

## Plan inclusion

Assess WCAG 2.2 AA, assistive technology, language, literacy, device, bandwidth and digital-confidence needs. Test sign-in and support, not only content pages. Provide a reasonable alternative for essential participation.

Explain accessibility accommodations for events and materials. Establish an accessible-content process for documents supplied by the agency.

## Establish administrative authority

Map sponsor, operational owner, membership administrator, publisher, moderator, support and assurance roles. Avoid shared privileged accounts. Align access with delegations and review it when staff or suppliers change.

Make official content visible. Define when staff participation represents the agency and when a matter needs to move into a formal process.

## Address information and records

Work with agency specialists to identify which content is a state record or business record, which system is authoritative, how capture occurs and what retention applies. Do not keep everything indefinitely by default.

Plan export with useful context and protected handling. Document what happens when the program ends.

## Assess privacy

Map identity, profile, discussion, support, moderation, analytics and operational information. Record purpose, access, disclosure, processor, location, retention and deletion.

Consider power imbalance. An invitation from government may feel compulsory. Use clear language and collect only what eligibility and program delivery require.

Reject unrelated behavioural advertising and cross-site profiling. Treat AI as a new purpose requiring assessment and authorisation.

## Procure security evidence

Match assurance to information sensitivity and agency policy. Review architecture, identity, access, secure development, logging, vulnerability management, recovery, incident response, encryption, suppliers and exit.

Verify certification scope and currency. Portal Page claims no government accreditation or security certification on this website.

## Prepare moderation and safety

Publish standards and reporting. Define severity, response, external escalation and moderator support. Separate community moderation from formal complaints and emergencies.

Record consequential decisions appropriately while protecting reporters and affected members.

## Run accountable review

Review the public purpose, membership, access, information, accessibility, incidents, reports, processors and measures regularly. Use metrics only where they inform a decision and collect them proportionately.
